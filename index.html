How to connect this to your Google Sheet

In Google Sheets, use File → Share → Publish to web, publish the schedule sheet, then choose CSV and paste that URL into the box above. Keep your columns in this order: Team Member, Service Type, Start Date, Check-In, End Date, Check-Out.

Once connected, the dashboard reads the sheet whenever you click Refresh from Google Sheet. Your sheet remains the master schedule; you do not maintain a second calendar.
